# Block mechanics · Episode 4 · What's the date?

1. **Month Flip** — Russian cue → choose one of four English months. The chosen card flips in 3D. A wrong card briefly reveals its real translation and flips back; a correct card stays open and can be listened to.
2. **Ordinal Lift** — cardinal number → choose the ordinal written form. The elevator car moves upward after the correct answer; pronunciation is available.
3. **Date Match** — four spoken-style date phrases on the left and four written dates on the right. Correct matches are joined by animated dashed lines. Two rounds; the right side is intentionally shuffled.
4. **Calendar Tap** — the learner hears / reads a date and taps the correct day on a real calendar grid.
5. **Sentence Builder** — the one sentence-building block in the module. Russian cue + shuffled English words + British English model.
6. **Phone English** — short phone dialogue, one comprehension question, optional hidden transcript and hint under the visual. TTS reads only the lines, not speaker names.
7. **Date Stamp** — convert a date between spoken and written form. Correct choice triggers a visual MATCHED stamp animation.
8. **Save the Date** — signature block. A short message contains a date; the learner independently sets the month and the day with two interactive controls, then saves the date. Correct selection creates a saved-state animation.
