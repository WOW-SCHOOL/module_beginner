# WOW SCHOOL · Beginner · Practical English · Episode 4 · What's the date?

Interactive post-lesson reinforcement module for students.

## Curriculum focus
- months;
- ordinal numbers;
- saying and reading dates;
- short practical phone English.

All exercise texts, mini-dialogues, prompts, examples and interactions in this archive are newly written for the WOW SCHOOL reinforcement module.

## 8 blocks
1. **Month Flip** — 3D flip cards: choose the English month from a Russian cue; the card flips and reveals the translation.
2. **Ordinal Lift** — choose the correct ordinal form; the lift moves to the correct floor after a successful answer.
3. **Date Match** — connect a spoken-style date with its written form; correct pairs are joined by animated lines.
4. **Calendar Tap** — hear/read a date and tap the correct day on an interactive calendar.
5. **Sentence Builder** — build one useful date / phone phrase from shuffled words; Russian cue + British English model.
6. **Phone English** — short practical phone dialogues with optional transcript under the visual. Names are visible in the transcript, but TTS reads only the lines.
7. **Date Stamp** — convert between spoken and written date formats; correct answers receive an animated stamp.
8. **Save the Date** — signature interaction: set month + day with two interactive controls and save the date from a short message.
