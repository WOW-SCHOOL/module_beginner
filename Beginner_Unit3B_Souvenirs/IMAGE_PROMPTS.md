Global style for every image: bright, juicy, realistic premium lifestyle photography; modern London travel atmosphere; adults / young adults; natural daylight; detailed and clean; no cartoon, no anime, no 3D, no embedded text, no logos, no readable prices, no random letters. Keep all important people, faces, hands and objects inside a 10–12% safe area. Images must work with **object-fit: contain** and must not need cropping.
