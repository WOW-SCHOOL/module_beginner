# WOW SCHOOL Module Rules

- Keep the overall WOW SCHOOL module structure unchanged.
- Final results page must stay simple, visual, and readable.
- British English TTS support is required.
- For Beginner modules, avoid long difficult listening at the very start.
- If a sentence-builder mechanic is used, keep it to **one block only** unless explicitly requested otherwise.
- The experimental block should still be understandable for a Beginner learner.
- Images are inserted later; placeholders and exact filenames must be preserved.

- In every task block, the right-hand task card must keep the feedback/status strip and internal progress visible at all times. Interactive content above it must shrink or scroll inside its own area; it must never push the status/progress below the card.
- No task content may overlap headings, instructions, controls, feedback, or progress. Long content must reflow, reduce within safe limits, or scroll inside its designated inner area.
- Lesson images must use `object-fit: contain`; never crop important content to fill the frame.
