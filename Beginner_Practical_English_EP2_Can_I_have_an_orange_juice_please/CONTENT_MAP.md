# Content Map

- **Block 1 — Understanding Prices:** read and choose prices in pounds, euros, and dollars.
- **Block 2 — Pronunciation Lab:** sort words by /ʊə/, /s/, and /k/.
- **Block 3 — Lunch Menu Explorer:** use a lunch menu to answer price questions.
- **Block 4 — Buying Lunch: Rob:** complete and understand Rob's dialogue in a pub.
- **Block 5 — Jenny & Amy:** follow the New York deli mini-dialogue and answer questions.
- **Block 6 — Memory Match:** match English useful phrases with Russian meanings.
- **Block 7 — Useful Phrases Quiz:** choose the best phrase in each situation.
