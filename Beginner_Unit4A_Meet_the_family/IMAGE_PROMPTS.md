# Image prompts — Beginner · Unit 4A · Meet the family

Global style for every image: bright, juicy, highly realistic premium lifestyle photography; warm, modern everyday atmosphere; adults and children in natural poses; clean composition; natural daylight or soft indoor daylight; detailed faces, hands, clothing and small objects; no cartoon, no anime, no 3D, no distorted anatomy, no extra fingers, no duplicated objects, no warped background details. Keep all important people, faces, hands and objects inside a **10–12% safe area**. Every image must work with **object-fit: contain** and must **not need cropping**. Visual variety is important: do **not** make all scenes look like the same photo session. Use different angles, locations, compositions and groups of people while keeping a consistent polished educational lifestyle look.

## 1) `preview-unit4a.jpg` — 1600×900, 16:9
Create a polished WOW SCHOOL cover image with **text baked into the image** in a clean premium educational promo style. Scene: a cheerful modern family moment or friendly family-introduction scene in London / Europe style, with one or two adults, one or two children, and a relaxed home / café / city-family atmosphere. Leave the visual subject mainly on the **right side**, and design the **left side** as a bright clean layout panel with elegant shapes and clear typography. Include these exact text elements, fully readable and beautifully arranged:
- "WOW SCHOOL"
- "online english school • wow-school.ru"
- "INTERACTIVE LESSON MODULE"
- "Unit 4A"
- "Meet the family"

- small feature labels or icons for: "Vocabulary", "Possessive adjectives", "Family Match", "Reading", "Listening", "Pronunciation"
- CTA button style text: "Let’s learn!"
The whole cover must look premium, modern, bright, and ready to use as the final preview image.
