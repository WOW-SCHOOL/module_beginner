# Image prompts — Beginner · Revise & Check 3&4

Create **9 separate images** for one interactive WOW SCHOOL English module. The visual language should feel like one premium 2026 educational campaign, but every scene must have a different location, composition, camera angle, and action.

## GLOBAL VISUAL STYLE

Bright, juicy, premium realistic lifestyle photography; modern 2026 visual language; believable people in natural candid poses; target audience teens and adults 16–45; crisp small details; realistic hands, faces, hair, clothing, phones, bags, cars, interiors, reflections and small objects. Natural daylight or soft premium indoor light. No staged stock-photo smiles, no plastic skin, no cartoon, no anime, no 3D render, no fake UI, no watermarks.

Important subjects must stay inside a **10–12% safe area**. Every image must work with **object-fit: contain** and must not require cropping. Do not place key faces, hands, objects, cars or family members at the very edges.

Avoid visible brand logos. Avoid readable random text, fake labels, fake prices, licence plates, and distorted typography. If text appears naturally in the environment, it must be too small / soft to matter.

The set must NOT look like the same two people photographed in the same café eight times. Vary:
- people and group size;
- indoor / outdoor locations;
- wide, medium, close, top-down, over-the-shoulder and selfie perspectives;
- clothing and time of day;
- family, travel, shopping, car and study contexts.
