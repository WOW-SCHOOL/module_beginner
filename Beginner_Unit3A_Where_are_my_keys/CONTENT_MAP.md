# CONTENT MAP — Beginner · Unit 3A · Where are my keys?

## Grammar
- singular and plural nouns
- a / an with singular nouns
- What is it? / What are they?
- It’s ... / They’re ...
- regular plural patterns represented in lesson examples: key → keys, watch → watches, dictionary → dictionaries
- glasses treated as plural

## Vocabulary
phone, watch, tablet, wallet / purse, pencil, notebook, glasses, photo, charger, ID card / passport, umbrella, camera, credit card / debit card, key, newspaper

## Pronunciation
Plural endings:
- /z/: bags, phones, keys, pens
- /s/: books, coats, passports, tablets
- /ɪz/: watches, glasses, pieces, purses

## Functional language / phrases
Oh no! · Where’s my phone? · Where are my glasses? · What is it? · What are they? · I think it’s ... · I think they’re ... · What’s in your bag? · I have two credit cards.
