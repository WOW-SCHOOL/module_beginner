# WOW SCHOOL · Beginner · Unit 4B · The perfect car

A full interactive module for the `module_beginner` repository.

## Blocks
1. Car Showroom Listening — 8 tasks + dialogue audio
2. Colours & Adjectives — 12 audio-supported tasks
3. Garage Sort — 12 drag / tap-to-place cards in 3 columns
4. Adjective Sentence Builder — 8 sentence-building tasks + model audio
5. Reading Mission — 6 questions + full text audio, designed for a 9:16 portrait image
6. Pronunciation & Linking — 10 audio tasks (/ɑː/, /ɔː/, linking)
7. Listening Mission — 8 questions across two dialogues
8. **NEW:** Test Drive Challenge — dice + moving car + 8 sentence-building checkpoints

## Important
- British-English synthesis is preserved; long speech is split into short chunks.
- Bottom task progress is pinned and must remain visible.
- Images are always displayed with `object-fit: contain`.
- Final lesson images are included in `assets/images/` and already connected to the corresponding blocks.
